# Track C workshop plan

AI for Career and Productivity | November 15, 2026 | 45 minutes

Status: consolidated print draft for coordinator review. Rehearsal and all-coordinator approval remain unrecorded.

## Purpose and delivery

Participants use AI on familiar work, define what good looks like, improve an output, and identify the human checks and permissions needed to repeat it. Success means a useful result and a method they can use on Monday.

Two back-to-back rounds, 2:15-3:00 pm and 3:00-3:45 pm. Each has three independent cohorts of about 30, one per coordinator. Every coordinator runs every segment. Section authors are preparation owners, not rotating presenters. Each cohort has three tables with a timekeeping captain. No slide deck required.

Use `final/facilitator-run-sheet.pdf` for coordinators. Editable sources and rebuild instructions are in `final/README.md`. Earlier worksheet exports in `final/worksheets/` are superseded.

## Preparation and participation

- Bring a charged phone, Muse installed and signed in on a personal account if available, and one appropriate weekly task. App download, email connection and payment are not conditions of participation.
- Personal inbox connection is optional and participant-controlled. Before connection, explain the actual access requested, including whether it covers more than selected messages. Do not promise read-only or per-message access unless offered. Never connect an employer account.
- Anyone declining email access may follow a willing neighbor. The owner holds the phone and chooses only non-sensitive content to show. Do not ask anyone to justify declining. If sharing is unsuitable, the same reasoning can be done on paper.
- No sending, purchasing, public sharing, deletion or live autonomous actions. Keep confidential work, credentials, financial records and identifying information out. A personal inbox can contain sensitive information about other people. Do not read private messages aloud.
- Before the event, test actual mobile sign-in, consent, briefing, document export and disconnect. Do not promise untested features. Export failure uses visible text or a saved note while preserving the exercise.

- **00-02 | Intro:** promise, house rules, willing-neighbor option, table captain. Page 1.
- **02-07 | Tool landscape:** categories, work examples, least access needed. Page 1.

- **42-45 | Wrap-up:** next step, measure and exit checks. Page 4.

## Facilitation priorities

- The source worksheet has five five-minute parts. The former agenda allowed 20 minutes; this agenda gives them 25. Landscape and prompting are five minutes each, guardrails five and wrap-up three.
- Email is the default, not compulsory. Keep non-email participants on their chosen task, then make a checklist, style guide or reusable reference. Map the chore worth repeating.
- Judge against the quality bar, not polish. Ask what was invented, omitted, too generic or unnecessary. A model's self-check is not independent verification. Comparing revisions from one app does not rank models.
- If left/right is awkward on a phone, label drafts A/B and record A as left, B as right. The owner controls what is visible. One voluntary sanitized share per table only if time allows.
- Protect task time and guardrails. Cut product tours, extra prompt rounds and table reports first. Aim for one useful result, not multiple polished exports.

## Evidence and completion

Hypothesis: completing familiar work, checking it against explicit criteria, and defining an approval boundary produces a practical next-week experiment.

Target: at least 80% of participants report a task and quality bar, one concrete revision with a measure, a human approval boundary, and a dated next step. Use a brief show of hands at the close; it is self-reported completion, not independently verified performance. People following a neighbor record their own reasoning. Distinguish generated, observed and paper-only participation.

Disconfirm success if most attendees only watch, cannot name a useful improvement, or believe inbox connection and all permissions are mandatory. Time saved is an estimate until measured including checking and correction.

Week 1: drafting. Week 2: summary or research with verification. Week 3: workflow map with approval. Week 4: repeat the best task and compare time, corrections or quality with the baseline. Share a sanitized result at work only if appropriate.
